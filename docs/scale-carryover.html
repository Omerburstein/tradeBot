Does the previous day's magnitude predict the next day's opening scale?

Each factor is normalized by meanAbs of its recent history, which needs 3 fresh Greek ticks — 30 minutes of wall clock. Seeding that scale from the previous day would remove the warm-up, but only if the previous day's magnitude actually predicts the next open. Each bar counts trading days whose ratio (previous-day magnitude ÷ current-day opening magnitude) fell in that bin. 1× is a perfect seed. Bins are one doubling wide.
